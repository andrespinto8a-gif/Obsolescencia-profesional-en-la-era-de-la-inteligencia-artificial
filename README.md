🎯 Risk Prioritization System
📌 Overview

This project implements a structured approach to risk prioritization based on impact and probability criteria. It helps classify risks into four treatment categories: Accept, Reduce, Avoid, and Share, facilitating better decision-making in risk management processes.

⚙️ Methodology

Risks are evaluated using two main criteria:

Probability (likelihood of occurrence)
Impact (severity of consequences)

A risk matrix is used to determine priority levels:

Low Risk → Accept
Medium Risk → Reduce
High Risk → Avoid or Share

Additionally, both qualitative and quantitative analyses were applied to strengthen the evaluation process.

📊 Results of the Research
🔍 Qualitative Analysis

The qualitative assessment identified the most critical risks based on expert judgment and categorization. Key findings include:

Operational risks were the most frequent.
Financial risks showed the highest potential impact.
External risks (market and suppliers) presented medium probability but high uncertainty.
📈 Quantitative Analysis

Through numerical evaluation of probability and impact:

High-risk scenarios were concentrated in processes with low control mechanisms.
Medium risks represented the majority, indicating opportunities for improvement rather than critical failure.
Low risks were minimal and considered acceptable without immediate action.
🔗 Integration of Results

By combining qualitative and quantitative approaches:

A more accurate prioritization of risks was achieved.
Decision-making improved by aligning subjective judgment with numerical data.
Risks were effectively classified into treatment strategies:
Accept: Low impact and low probability risks
Reduce: Moderate risks requiring mitigation controls
Avoid: High impact risks that can be eliminated
Share: High risks transferred through external mechanisms (e.g., insurance)
🛠️ Risk Treatment Strategies
Accept: No action required, risk is tolerable.
Reduce: Implement controls to minimize impact or likelihood.
Avoid: Eliminate the activity causing the risk.
Share: Transfer risk (e.g., insurance or outsourcing).
🚀 Usage
Identify risks.
Assign probability and impact scores.
Classify using the risk matrix.
Apply appropriate treatment strategy.
